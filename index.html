          The companion — a separate app

          Experiment: a second extension that adds pictures and word maps.

          Experiment is a separate extension you install alongside Not a Translator. Once both are present, Experiment plugs into the very same definition tooltip and adds three things when they make sense: an illustrative picture, a graph of meanings (SenseMap), and a graph of a word's family and origin (RootMap). It is offered as a thank-you to the people who support the project.

          A picture, when it helps

          For a plant, an animal, an object or a place, Experiment can show an illustrative photo right next to the definition — sourced from open image libraries, never a distraction you did not ask for.

          SenseMap — explore meaning

          A small, draggable graph around the word: synonyms, antonyms, broader and narrower terms. Tap any node to read its definition, expand it, and keep exploring the semantic neighbourhood.

          RootMap — family & origin

          A sister graph for the word's family: derived words and relatives grow upward, while the etymons — the older forms it comes from — plunge downward like roots.

          The grouping terms, in plain words

          Synonym
          a word with almost the same meaning — big / large.
          Antonym
          a word with the opposite meaning — big / small.
          Hypernym
          a broader, umbrella term — dog → animal.
          Hyponym
          a more specific case — animal → dog. Grouped with hypernyms, above.
          Meronym
          a part of something — wheel → car.
          Holonym
          the whole that owns the part — car → wheel. Grouped with meronyms, below.
          Derived word
          built from the same base — friend → friendship, friendly.
          Related word
          same lexical family, not a direct derivation — nation → native.
          Etymon
          an older form the word comes from — friend → Old English frēond.

          SenseMap — colours & positions

          Around the central word, each family of meaning has a fixed place and its own colour. The palette is a tetrad built from your tooltip colour (violet by default), so the map always matches your chosen theme.

          • Synonyms — to the right (your tooltip colour).
          • Antonyms — to the left (opposite hue).
          • Hypernyms (broader terms) — above.
          • Meronyms / holonyms (parts & wholes) — below.

          RootMap — colours & positions

          RootMap reads vertically, like a tree. The word's family fans out above it; its origins sink below it. Derived and related words follow your tooltip colour, while the etymons keep a fixed earthy beige — the soil the roots reach into.

          • Derived words — upper-left.
          • Related words — upper-right.
          • Etymons (older forms it comes from) — below, in beige.

          Both maps use the same engine: drag a node to move its branch, tap the star / tree icon on an open node to expand it, and use the double-arrow to swap between the quick Wiktionary definition and the full dictionary definition.

          FAQ

          A few things worth saying clearly.

          The tooltip toolbar — what each button does

          Not every button shows every time — each appears only when it applies, so a given tooltip shows a subset. The last three (image, SenseMap, RootMap) are added by Experiment.

          • Reposition (the cross of arrows) — rotates the tooltip around the word (below, left, above, right) to escape a screen edge or an overlap.
          • Translate anyway / Show original (the four-leaf clover) — swaps the native-language definition for a translation, and back. The definition always comes first; translation is the fallback.
          • Switch dictionary source (the double arrow) — toggles between the two sources for the same word (for example Larousse and Le Robert), when both are available.
          • Pronunciation — plays the audio when available. A small face shows the source: a human face for a real recording (Wiktionary, Commons, DWDS), a robot face for a synthetic voice (Google TTS).
          • More… (open in a new tab) — opens the source page (or a web search) for the word, when you want to dig further.
          • Repair → console (the wrench) — sends this lookup into console mode, the stable side panel used when a page fights inline rendering.
          • Remove (circle with a slash) — closes the tooltip. Nothing is left on the page; a refresh (F5) always restores it.
          • Image Experiment — shows an illustrative picture (or a small map for a place). Greyed out when nothing suitable is found — better no image than a misleading one.
          • SenseMap Experiment — opens the meaning graph. Appears only when the word has usable relations.
          • RootMap Experiment — opens the family & origin graph. Appears only when the word has a documented family.
