Privacy Policy

Not a Translator — Experiment (browser extension) · Last updated: July 2026

In short: This extension is a dictionary tool. It sends only the single word you hover or select to public dictionary and image services to fetch its definition, related words and an illustrative picture. It does not collect your name, email, accounts, browsing history, or location. It does not track you, show ads, or sell any data.

What the extension does

Not a Translator — Experiment is a companion extension that enriches the definition tooltip of the Not a Translator dictionary: it can show an illustrative image, a graph of related meanings (SenseMap) and a graph of the word's family and origin (RootMap). All of these are triggered by the single word you hover over or select on a web page.

What data is processed

To perform a lookup, the extension transmits only the individual word you choose to look up (a piece of website text content) to the third-party services listed below. It does not send the page you are on, its URL, the surrounding text, your identity, or any other content.

What is NOT collected

  • No personally identifiable information (name, email, age, ID).
  • No accounts, passwords, or authentication data.
  • No financial or payment information.
  • No browsing history, list of visited pages, or page URLs.
  • No location, GPS, or IP-based geolocation is requested or used.
  • No clicks, keystrokes, mouse tracking, or on-page analytics.
  • No advertising, no user profiling, no sale or sharing of data with data brokers.

Third-party services contacted

Each service receives only the looked-up word, and only for the feature it powers:

  • Wiktionary (wiktionary.org) — definitions, synonyms/antonyms, word family and etymology.
  • notatr-proxy (a Cloudflare Worker operated by the developer) — caches image lookups and holds the API credentials for the image providers below, so those credentials never reside in your browser.
  • Unsplash and Openverse — illustrative photographs, retrieved through the notatr-proxy worker.
  • iNaturalist, GBIF, Wikidata, Wikimedia Commons — photographs of species and objects.
  • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim — map thumbnails for place names.
  • LINDAT / UDPipe (lindat.mff.cuni.cz) — reduces a word to its base form (lemmatization).

These services receive the request under their own privacy policies. As with any web request, they technically see the originating IP address; the extension neither adds nor stores it. Requests carry no identifier that would tie a lookup to you.

Caching

To keep lookups fast, results may be cached — in your browser's memory for the current session, and on the developer's Cloudflare edge / cache server. This cache is keyed by the word only, is fully anonymous, and is never linked to any user identity.

Data stored on your device

The extension uses chrome.storage.local solely to remember your own settings — four on/off toggles (extension enabled, images, SenseMap, RootMap). These settings stay on your device and are never transmitted.

Donations

Donations are entirely optional and are handled by external providers (PayPal, Buy Me a Coffee) outside the extension. If you email us after donating, we use your email address only to reply and to send the install link — never for marketing, and it is not shared.

Children

The extension is a general-audience dictionary tool and is not directed at children, nor does it knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

This policy may be updated as features evolve; the “Last updated” date above will change accordingly.
